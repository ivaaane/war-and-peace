# War and Peace, a word a day

just a dumb idea i had at midnight lmao

Basically, a Mastodon bot that posts one word from War and Peace by Leo Tolstoy once a day in order. Based on Peanuts' comic strip of [March 27th, 1972](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/peanuts/images/5/58/March_27th_1972.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/1000?cb=20221115020251).

The bot is at [@war_and_peace@mastodon.social](https://mastodon.social/@war_and_peace) btw.

Attributions to Terence Eden for his tutorial [Easy guide to building Mastodon bots](https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2018/08/easy-guide-to-building-mastodon-bots/).
